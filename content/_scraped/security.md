## Your data, protected.
1
### Client isolation
Customer data and research remain isolated from other client environments.
2
### Controlled connections
Your firm controls which sources Reflexivity can connect to and can revoke access at any time.
3
### Limited data retention
Reflexivity does not persistently store client data, minimizing exposure of sensitive information.
4
### Never trained on your data
Your prompts, documents, connected data, and outputs are never used to train Reflexivity’s models or those of third-party providers.
