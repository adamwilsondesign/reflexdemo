## Three rules every answer follows.
### Cited by default
Every answer links to the documents and data behind it, so any claim can be checked at its source.
### NA over guesswork
When data is missing, the answer shows NA rather than filling the gap with an estimate.
### Code-backed calculations
Calculations run in code, so every figure can be re-run and checked.
## Follow any claim back to its source.
Every answer keeps its evidence attached, from the source document through the calculation to the final claim. A reviewer can open each step and see where a figure came from.
1
The source document
The filing, transcript or data feed the figure came from, linked.
2
The calculation
The code that turned the source data into a figure, ready to re‑run.
3
The claim
The statement in the answer, with its source and calculation still attached.
## When you say Apple, it knows which Apple.
Apple Inc and Apple Hospitality REIT share a name, not a business. Reflexivity resolves every company to a single entity, knows which provider to query for it, and reconciles the data when sources disagree.
## A general model works with what you give it.
### A general model
It reasons over the documents and data you provide. Your team handles the sourcing, the entity matching and the checks on what comes back.
### Reflexivity
It maps every entity, enforces sourcing, flags missing data and keeps the workflow running, so your team starts from analysis it can check.
