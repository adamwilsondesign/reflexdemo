## Five kinds of research, one way of working.
### Investment research
Build a cited view of a company, sector or theme from one question.
### Portfolio analysis
Check holdings and exposures, with sources attached.
### Deep fundamental research
Work through one name’s filings, transcripts and data.
### Thesis pressure-testing
Test a thesis against the latest evidence before acting.
### Scenario analysis and backtesting
Run a scenario or backtest, with calculations kept for review.
