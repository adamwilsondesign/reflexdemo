Start with the finished Platform, or bring the Knowledge Graph, Alfred and Insights into the systems you already run.
Request a Demo
## Four ways to deploy Reflexivity.
Pick the option that fits how your team works. You can also combine Platform Access with a custom integration.
### Platform Access
Alfred, the Knowledge Graph, integrated data, Insights and Microsoft 365 in one maintained environment, for teams that don’t want to run the infrastructure.
### API & MCP
Keep the tools you already run, and plug in the layer they lack: entity resolution, the Knowledge Graph, monitoring and auditability.
### Knowledge Feeds
License the Knowledge Graph’s relationship data as a feed for thematic baskets, research platforms and your own internal models.
### Embedded
Offer Reflexivity intelligence as native functionality inside your own platform. It already runs this way in production with a partner.
## One foundation, however you deploy.
Every option runs on the same Knowledge Graph, the same data and the same auditability. Entity resolution keeps companies consistent across sources, every claim links to its source, and gaps in the data are flagged rather than filled.
