# Models will keep improving, so we build what lasts around them.
We build the parts that stay the same as models change: the data, the Knowledge Graph, the monitoring and the audit trail. Every answer starts from sources, and every claim links back to one.
### The data
Institutional data is integrated into the platform, so every answer starts from sources rather than guesses.
### The Knowledge Graph
Relationships are stored, not invented at question time. The Graph maps how companies connect through products, customers, competitors, themes and geographies.
### The monitoring
Insights watches the holdings and themes you follow for catalysts, earnings and theme shifts, and each item says why it appeared.
### The audit trail
Sources and code stay when the model changes. Every claim links to its source, and missing data is flagged rather than filled.
